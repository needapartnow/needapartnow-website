HAAS G13 CIRCULAR POCKET

7.875 diameter × .250 deep

This example uses a .500-inch center-cutting end mill. X0 Y0 is the pocket center and Z0 is the top of the part.

Before you run it

  • On a Haas Classic control, press SETTING/GRAPH, go to Setting 40, and set it to DIAMETER.
  • Enter .5000 in the D geometry offset that matches the tool in the spindle. D#3026 selects that matching offset automatically.
  • K is the finished pocket radius. Change it to one-half of your required finished diameter.
  • For a full pocket with another cutter, make I greater than the cutter radius but smaller than the cutter diameter, and keep Q smaller than the cutter diameter. A larger I is valid when you only want to cut near the outside, such as kissing jaws, but it will leave the center uncleared.
  • Change the tool number, spindle speed, feedrate, pocket depth, and safe positions for your machine, cutter, material, and setup.

Verify every value for your machine and setup. Run Graphics, then single-block it above the part before cutting.
